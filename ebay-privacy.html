Catalogue privacy notice

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Our catalogue connects to Pawfecthome’s eBay seller account so you can browse our products and follow a link to buy them on eBay.

This notice explains how Pawfecthome uses information through that connection. It covers the catalogue integration, rather than every aspect of our business or eBay’s own services. For questions about this notice, email Support@pawfecthome.co.uk.

Information we use

  • Product details: listing titles, descriptions, SKUs, prices, availability, variations, image links and eBay listing links. These are used to build the public catalogue.
  • Seller connection details: eBay account identifiers are processed when we connect our own seller account. Hashed identifiers are kept privately to match account-deletion notifications. Hashing does not make these identifiers anonymous.
  • Authorisation details: access tokens allow the catalogue to communicate with eBay. The stored refresh token is encrypted. The integration does not store the seller’s eBay password.
  • Service information: sync times, connection status and notification verification results help us keep the catalogue working. Our hosting provider may also process technical information, such as IP addresses and request details, to deliver and protect the service.

The catalogue integration does not import customer orders, delivery addresses or payment details. If you buy a product, checkout takes place on eBay.

Why we use it

We use this information to display our listings, update prices and availability, protect the seller connection and handle eBay account-deletion notifications. Where this involves personal information, our purpose is our legitimate interest in operating and securing our own shop catalogue. We do not use information received through this integration for advertising profiles or sell it to others.

Who handles the information

eBay supplies the account and listing information. Cloudflare hosts the website and catalogue service and stores the integration’s private settings and connection information. Public product details appear on our website; authorisation tokens and account-matching identifiers do not.

Product images may load directly from eBay. Opening those images or following an eBay listing link sends a request to eBay, which can receive technical information such as your IP address. eBay and Cloudflare operate internationally; their handling of information is described in the eBay privacy notice and Cloudflare privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep the seller connection information while it is needed to run the catalogue. Product information is refreshed by successful catalogue updates. If eBay sends a verified account-deletion notification matching the connected seller, the service removes the stored seller identifiers, refresh token and catalogue data. Cached copies can take a short time to clear.

The service keeps limited operational status information to diagnose connection problems. Please contact us if you want the connection information removed. Revoking access in eBay stops future authorised access but does not, by itself, remove every stored copy.

Cookies

The seller connection process uses a short-lived security cookie to protect sign-in. This privacy page does not set analytics or advertising cookies. eBay applies its own cookie policy when you visit its website.

Your choices and rights

You can contact us to ask about personal information handled by this integration, request access or correction, or ask for deletion, restriction or object to its use. A right to portability may also apply in some circumstances. These rights depend on the information and the reason it is processed.

Email Support@pawfecthome.co.uk. You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page if the way the catalogue uses information changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
